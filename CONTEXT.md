## 6. Research

- Doctorate: Education Policy, Institute of Education, HSE University (National Research University Higher School of Economics), Moscow. Started November 2023. Programme completed October 2026, pre-defense passed, final defense ahead.
- Dissertation: "Socio-Cultural and Institutional Conditions of Implementing Inclusive Education in Osun State, Nigeria". Supervisor: Prof. Sergey Kosaretsky.
- Subject: how obligations under Article 24 of the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) are translated into school practice in Nigeria.
- Fieldwork: qualitative study across five schools in Osun State (three private, two state-run), 48 adult participants and 15 learner narratives, December 2024. Ethics approval obtained; access negotiated with state officials, school leadership and community gatekeepers.
- Interviews conducted, transcribed and analysed in Yoruba and English with no translator.
- Policy document analysis across three levels: international convention, Nigerian federal policy, Osun State implementation.
- Two original concepts:
  - Culturally Negotiated Inclusion (CNI): how communities reshape inclusion rather than simply failing to implement it.
  - Advocacy Literacy: the competence through which families (mothers in particular) claim entitlements that exist on paper.
- Finding stated on the existing site: Nigeria has written the CRPD into law, but the law carries no education-specific enforcement, so in many schools inclusion stays on paper.
