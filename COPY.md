Heading (H2): The law says every child. The classroom often says otherwise.

That gap is where I work.

### Hero

Headline (H1): Inclusive education policy research, from the convention to the classroom

Opening: My research on inclusive education policy in Nigeria asks one question: why do commitments made in law stall before they reach children? I follow CRPD Article 24 through three levels, the international convention, Nigerian federal policy and Osun State implementation, to see where it holds and where it breaks.

### The doctoral study

Heading (H2): Five schools in Osun State

Dissertation: "Socio-Cultural and Institutional Conditions of Implementing Inclusive Education in Osun State, Nigeria". Education Policy, Institute of Education, HSE University. Supervisor: Prof. Sergey Kosaretsky.

- Qualitative fieldwork in five schools, three private and two state-run, in December 2024
- 48 adult participants and 15 learner narratives
- Interviews run, transcribed and analysed in Yoruba and English, with no translator in between
- Ethics approval, with access negotiated with state officials, school leadership and community gatekeepers
- Policy document analysis at international, federal and state level

Status: doctoral programme completed October 2026. Pre-defense passed, final defense ahead.

### Two ideas from the study

Heading (H2): Two ideas this research adds

**Culturally Negotiated Inclusion.** Communities do not simply fail to implement inclusion. They reshape it.

**Advocacy Literacy.** The competence families, mothers in particular, build to claim entitlements for their children that exist only on paper.
